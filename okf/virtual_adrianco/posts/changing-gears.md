I knew people at eBay from the time in 1999 when they had a big outage and many Sun people got involved in helping them get up and running again. I also figured out a lot about how eBay and PayPal really work, and the challenges of scaling a rapidly growing and changing high availability transactional platform to a size that is beyond most people's comprehension.
