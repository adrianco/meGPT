About eighteen months ago I<a href="http://perfcap.blogspot.com/2007/02/skype-downloads-approach-500-million.html"> wrote a blog post about Skype</a> as it approached 500 million downloads. They have continued at around a million a day, and now, nearly 500 days later we are coming up on the one billion mark.
