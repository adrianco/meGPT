<div xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"><p>The essential characteristics of the http based web are that by default everyone is anonymous, and everyone can get to everything. Its "free" and the trend is for the parts that are not free and anonymous to move in that direction. For example, you can now buy stuff on <a href="http://www.ebayexpress.com">eBay Express</a> without having to sign up for an eBay account, and there are fewer newspaper sites requiring paid subscriptions, since they are losing audience to the free sites.</p>  <p>However, the essential characteristics of the Skype peer to peer network are the opposite of the Internet. Everyone has a clear identity and no-one can get to anything without asking for permission or being invited. I think this truly a different paradigm for building systems.<br/> </p>  <p>Everyone on Skype is plugged into the public key encryption infrastructure (PKI) which provides a secure identity as well as secure communications between peers.
