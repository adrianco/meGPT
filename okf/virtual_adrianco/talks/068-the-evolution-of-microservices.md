# The Evolution of Microservices

Adrian Cockcroft, Technology Fellow at Battery Ventures

The Evolution of Microservices

We’ve come a long way over the last few years, what started as a mixture of fine grain service oriented architectures, run-what-you-wrote and infrastructure as a service has matured into predominantly open source, containerized, microservice based architecture concepts, DevOps and cloud native deployments. Along the way, the implementation language of choice for new applications and tools has shifted from Java-centric to Go centric, new tools have appeared and a whole new ecosystem has developed. Far more responsibility is being placed on the developer, to optimize for time-to-value of hardened secure applications, while managing self-service auto-scaled capacity efficiently. I’ll talk about the underlying trends that are fueling this change, how people are migrating in practice, and where we are headed next. --
