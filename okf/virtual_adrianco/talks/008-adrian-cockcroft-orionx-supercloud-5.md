# Adrian Cockcroft, OrionX | Supercloud 5

Adrian Cockcroft, partner and analyst at #OrionX, speaks with #theCUBEResearch analysts John Furrier and Dave Vellante at the #Supercloud5 event. They discuss #AWS' efforts in the #RenewableEnergy domain and the broader challenges that cloud providers face in achieving #sustainability goals. 

Get insights into what you might have missed at Supercloud 5: The Battle for AI Supremacy 
https://siliconangle.com/2023/12/06/three-insights-might-missed-supercloud-5-battle-ai-supremacy-event-supercloud5/

As a part of the #GreenSoftwareFoundation, Cockcroft is spearheading the “Realtime Cloud” project, focusing on providing real-time energy and carbon standards for cloud providers.Cockcroft highlights the lack of transparency and the difficulty in comparing sustainability metrics across different #cloud providers, emphasizing the need for a unified framework or schema for sustainability metrics and the importance of #RealTimeData in making informed decisions.
