# Innovating at speed : AWS and Formula 1

Adrian Cockcroft speaks on how Formula 1 is a data-driven sport. During each race, 120 sensors on each car generate 3 GB of data, and 1,500 data points are generated each second. Using Amazon SageMaker, Formula 1’s data scientists are training deep-learning models with 65 years of historical race data to extract critical race performance statistics, make race predictions, and give fans insight into the split-second decisions and strategies adopted by teams and drivers. Join this talk to see how AWS and Formula 1 are innovating at speed.
