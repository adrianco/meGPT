# AWS Executive Forum 2019 - Positioning Large Transformation Efforts to the Board

Presented at AWS Executive Forum 2019 in Sydney.
Positioning Large Transformation Efforts to the Board
Visit https://amzn.to/2FrVHZS for more thought leadership content.

Board and Executive Level sponsorship is proven to accelerate and broaden the impact of cloud migration. However change strategies that are driven top down require a particular approach. During this session we will explore the best practices in positioning large transformation efforts to the board.

Speakers
- Adrian Cockcroft, Vice President Cloud Architecture Strategy, AWS,
- Rob Smedley, Technical Consultant, F1
