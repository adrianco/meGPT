# Adrian Cockcroft Presents: The Startup Lifecycle

In this video from the StartupHPC Meetup at SC14, Adrian Cockcroft from Battery Ventures presents: The Startup Lifecycle.

Visionary Battery Ventures Technology Fellow Adrian Cockcroft’s provocative “Startup Lifecycle” gives birth to a quintessential insiders guide in creating a successful and memorable debut. Cockcroft espouses the what’s and who’s and how to capture and keep the attention of the VC community. Bonus material includes emerging opportunity and challenging conventions in IP monetization.
